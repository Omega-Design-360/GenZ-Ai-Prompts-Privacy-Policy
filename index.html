We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use data such as:

This information is used to deliver and personalize ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. AdMob's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
