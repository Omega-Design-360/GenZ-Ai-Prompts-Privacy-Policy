Privacy Policy

Genz AI: AI Photos & Prompts · Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Genz AI: AI Photos & Prompts ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

In short

Data collection in this app is optional and limited to what's needed to serve ads. We never sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

Information We Collect

Genz AI: AI Photos & Prompts does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number directly.

The app displays AI-generated images alongside their prompts and is supported by advertising through Google AdMob. Any data collection tied to advertising is optional — you can limit or opt out of it at any time (see "Your Choices & Opt-Out" below), and doing so will not prevent you from using the app's core features.

Advertising Partners

The app is supported by advertising served through two third-party ad networks: Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Where permitted by your device settings, these partners may collect and use data such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • Ad interaction and usage data

This information is used to deliver and personalize ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Each partner's handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy:

Google AdMob

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Unity Ads (Unity Technologies)

Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or device data to any third party, under any circumstances. The limited data described above is shared only with our advertising partners (Google AdMob and Unity Ads) strictly for the purpose of serving and measuring ads within the app, and never for a separate commercial purpose of our own.

Your Choices & Opt-Out

Data collection for advertising purposes is optional, and you're always free to limit ad personalization or reset/delete your advertising identifier from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"

These settings apply across both AdMob and Unity Ads, since both networks read the same device-level advertising identifier and tracking permission. Opting out may result in ads that are less relevant to you, but it will not limit your access to any feature of the app.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Security

We rely on trusted third-party services (such as Google) that maintain industry-standard security practices. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
